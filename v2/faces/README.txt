face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx (YuNet face detector, 232,589 bytes)
Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
Authors: Wu et al., "YuNet: A Tiny Millisecond-level Face Detector" (2023). Licence: MIT.
