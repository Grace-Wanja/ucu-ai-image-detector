Is that photo real?

AI can now make a convincing photo in seconds. Drop in a picture and Mazima tells you whether it looks AI-generated, and how sure it is.

Your photo never leaves your phone or computer. The check runs inside your browser.

Getting Mazima ready. The first visit downloads the model (about 94 MB), so use Wi-Fi if you can.

RealAI-generated

    How it works

    1. Choose a photo. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 25 MB.
    2. Mazima looks at it. A trained model scans five patches of the picture for the faint patterns AI tools leave behind.
    3. You get a verdict. Likely real, likely AI-generated, or not sure, with the score shown so you can judge for yourself.

    How far to trust it

    Mazima is a screening aid, not proof. It can be wrong, including when it sounds sure, so check anything important with a second source.

    • Photos shared through WhatsApp or screenshots are squeezed and shrunk, which makes the check less reliable.
    • Very small or heavily edited pictures are harder to judge.
    • It looks at still images only. It does not check video or face swaps.
    • It does not point to the part of the picture that looks fake, because our tests showed such heat maps were not reliable.

    Mazima is a student research prototype from Uganda Christian University. It asks a simple question: how well do AI image detectors work on Ugandan photos?
